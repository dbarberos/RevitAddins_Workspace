## 1. Summary of Changes

In this work cycle on branch `TransferTransformBy`, we completed the specification, UI integration, mutual exclusivity logic, and telemetry for the three **"Transform By"** options under the On Views & Geometry section in **TransferPlus**:

1. **XAML Tooltips & Grouping ([TransferPlusView.xaml](file:///c:/Users/david.barbero/Documents/DOCUMENTOS/ALTEN/Workbench/RevitAddins_Workspace/RevitAddins_Workspace/TransferPlus/Views/TransferPlusView.xaml)):**
