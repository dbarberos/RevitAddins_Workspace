# FilterPlus - Professional Selection and Filtering for Autodesk® Revit®

## The Selection Revolution in BIM
**FilterPlus** is not just another selection tool; it is a meticulously engineered data management engine designed to bridge the gap between complex BIM data and intuitive user interaction. In massive-scale architectural, engineering, and construction projects, finding the exact elements you need can be like searching for a needle in a haystack. FilterPlus transforms this process into a seamless, high-speed navigation experience.

### 🚀 Advanced Features for Power Users

#### 1. Comprehensive 5-Level Hierarchy Browser
Stop clicking through multiple menus to find your elements. Our hierarchical tree view provides a unified display of:
- **Project Root**: Access everything in the current scope.
- **Category Level**: Standard Revit categories organized for clarity.
- **Family Level**: Instant visualization of all families loaded in the model.
- **Type Level**: Granular control over specific family types.
- **Element ID Level**: Direct access to individual Revit instances.

#### 2. Semantic Depth Memory (SDM) Technology
One of the biggest pain points in Revit add-ins is losing your navigation progress when you re-sort or refresh. FilterPlus features a proprietary **Semantic Depth Memory** system. Whether you are switching from sorting by Level to sorting by Phase, the application remembers exactly which branches you had expanded, keeping your focus where it belongs and saving hundreds of clicks per session.

#### 3. Dynamic Multi-Criteria Organization
Re-order your project data on the fly. FilterPlus allows you to instantly restructure the selection tree based on:
- **Phase of Creation**: Essential for renovation projects and construction sequencing.
- **Level/Altitude**: Perfect for floor-by-floor coordination and clash detection preparation.
- **Workset Allocation**: A must-have for collaborative projects where multi-user coordination is key.

#### 4. Intelligent Search Engine with Regex Support
Our search bar isn't a simple text filter. It is a professional-grade search utility:
- **Regular Expressions (Regex)**: Use complex patterns to find specific naming conventions (e.g., all walls starting with 'EXT' and ending with '01').
- **Logical OR Operations**: Accumulate selection results from multiple searches. Search for 'Doors', then search for 'Windows' with the OR toggle, and see both highlighted in your tree.
- **Name-Only Optimization**: Toggle this mode to skip complex parameter checks and achieve lightning-fast results in projects with over 100,000 elements.

### 💎 Streamlined Workflow Benefits

- **Massive Time Savings**: Replace the cumbersome native Revit filter with a modern interface that allows you to select, isolate, and manage thousands of elements in seconds.
- **Precision Auditing**: Quickly identify elements that are on the wrong level, phase, or workset by simply switching the organization mode.
- **Improved Performance**: Built with high-performance WPF virtualization, FilterPlus remains responsive even when handling the largest "hospital-scale" or "infrastructure-scale" Revit models.
- **Visual Intelligence**: Checkboxes support "Indeterminate" states (square check), providing immediate visual feedback when only some elements within a family or type are selected.

### 🛠 Technical Specifications and Stability
FilterPlus is built on the latest **Nice3point Revit Toolkit**, ensuring industry-leading stability and compatibility.
- **Multi-Version Support**: Fully compatible with Revit 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026, and 2027.
- **Modern Tech Stack**: Utilizes .NET Framework 4.8 for legacy stability and .NET 8.0 for cutting-edge performance in Revit 2025+.
- **Thread-Safe Architecture**: Selection and filtering operations are performed in safe execution contexts, preventing Revit "spinning wheels" or unexpected crashes.
