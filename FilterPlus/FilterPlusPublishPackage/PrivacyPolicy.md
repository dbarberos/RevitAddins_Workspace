# Privacy Policy - FilterPlus

## Data Collection
**FilterPlus** does not collect, store, or transmit any personal, user, or project data outside of your local Revit environment. All filtering and selection operations are performed in-memory within your workstation.

## Third Parties
We do not share data with any third parties. The add-in does not contain analytics tools, advertising networks, or third-party SDKs that capture information.

## Retention and Deletion
Since no data is collected, there is no retention policy. Any user settings (UI preferences) are stored locally in your user profile (`%AppData%`) and are removed upon uninstalling the plug-in.

## Revocation of Consent
Use of the add-in implies acceptance of these local privacy conditions. If you wish to "revoke" access, simply uninstall the plug-in from the Windows Control Panel.
